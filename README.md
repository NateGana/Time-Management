# ⏱ ChronoFocus

**Master Your Time, Own Your Focus.**

ChronoFocus is a distraction-free productivity workspace that combines a Pomodoro timer, a time-block task manager, a daily focus tracker, and ambient background sound — all in one clean, dark-themed interface. No installs, no sign-ups, no tracking. Open it and start focusing.

🔗 **[Launch ChronoFocus →](#)**
https://nategana.github.io/Time-Management/

---

## ✨ What You Get

### 🍅 Pomodoro Timer
A focus timer built around the proven Work → Break rhythm:
- **25-minute Work** sessions, **5-minute Short Breaks**, and **15-minute Long Breaks** (every 4th session)
- Fully customizable durations for each mode
- A glowing progress ring so you always know how much time is left at a glance
- A sound + on-screen flash the moment a session ends — plus an optional browser notification, so you never lose track even if the tab is in the background
- Optional **auto-start**, so sessions can flow into each other without you lifting a finger

### 🗂 Time-Block Task Manager
Plan your day, not just your to-do list:
- Add tasks with a name, a category, and an estimated time block
- Check tasks off as you complete them
- See your total queued minutes at a glance, so you know if today's plan is realistic
- Clear completed tasks with one click

### 📊 Daily Focus Tracker
A quiet dashboard that keeps you honest:
- **Pomodoros completed today**
- **Total focus hours today**
- **Current streak** — consecutive days you've shown up
- **All-time pomodoro count**
- Everything is saved automatically in your browser and is still there the next time you open the page — even after a refresh or a restart

### 🌗 Terminal / Cyberpunk Theme
A sleek, high-contrast dark interface inspired by developer terminals, with neon cyan and green accents. A one-click toggle switches to a lighter, high-contrast alternative for daytime use or personal preference.

### 🎧 Ambient Focus Sound
Three background soundscapes generated live in your browser — no audio files, no downloads:
- **Rain** — soft, evolving white noise
- **Cafe** — a low murmur with the occasional distant clink
- **Lofi Tick** — a warm, steady tick to keep pace by

Adjust the volume or turn it off entirely — whatever helps you lock in.

## 🔒 Privacy First

ChronoFocus runs **entirely inside your browser**. There is no backend, no database, no account, and nothing is ever sent over the network. Your tasks and your focus stats live only in your browser's local storage, on your device. Clear your browser data, and it resets — that's the only way it goes away.

## 🚀 Getting Started

There's nothing to install.

1. Open the app in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari).
2. Pick a timer mode and hit **Start**.
3. Add your tasks for the day in the Time-Block panel.
4. Optionally turn on an ambient sound.
5. Come back tomorrow and watch your streak grow.

That's it — no setup screen, no configuration file, no dependencies.

## 🖥 Works Everywhere

ChronoFocus is fully responsive and adapts to whatever screen you're using it on — desktop, tablet, or phone — so your focus routine isn't tied to one device.

---

## 🛠 Under the Hood (For the Curious)

ChronoFocus is built as a **single self-contained HTML file** — no frameworks, no build step, no external libraries. Ambient sound is synthesized live using the Web Audio API, and all your data is stored using `localStorage`. That means it's also easy to self-host: download the file and open it, or drop it on any static web host.
